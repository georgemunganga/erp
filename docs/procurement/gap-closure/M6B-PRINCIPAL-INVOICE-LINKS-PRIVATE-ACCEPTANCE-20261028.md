## Failures, cleanup, and limits

The first private fixture setup stopped at a too-broad supplier-invoice UPDATE guard and rolled back atomically; all 223 business and security snapshots matched before state (SHA `ba92041a2838e62174145441da532f09b39b9acdd1a3e8a4df2fefa6d4a1bae2`). The corrected fixture used nine named UPDATE columns. The first invoice intake returned 503 because Record lacked `purchase_orders` SELECT; the owner confirmed no invoice persisted while the published policy remained (SHA `fa038f0b2691413b5d04fb4fc07992a86a3a20690e99b364995009693ba9f102`). An exact private SELECT-only extension (SHA `b6a122459d23462cf90ca9427e0912ba0c93f7274e9e89b8c5a21f5273fd5b43`) permitted the continuation. The original UI fixture initially loaded demo assets until a genuine live-mode build was used; later a Radix chooser waiter needed the actual rendered control. Those prewrite failures and the failed intake are retained separately, without relabeling them as passes.

The flag-off API and static proxy stopped cleanly (SHAs `7fee61840b71f1cd7ef892617023889da61056832fe15dae53bd8914c9d9211a` and `5eb274e2aa2c153e056becaafaafb26e4552bd4d8e7492024394a51fe505a4cf`). The temporary Record SELECT extension was restored first (SHA `973a0f0ce7da1927a2a323a215ed663649a4f28f6198364421067ce66f0ac115`); then the synthetic role and ACL fixture was restored (SHA `6533d8325fc27c9927fc3fd7d4f92474c2d670b75c8f328e82c33c93c50546ef`), with zero active synthetic sessions. Legitimate policy, invoice, mapping, receipt, and audit rows remain in the **private** clone, so the fixture restore does not claim the full business database returned to its earlier contents.

This acceptance covers one HQ Goods source and four workerless ordinary-cookie actors; it does not prove excluded-branch denial, concurrent capacity races, a full independent crypto or new-auth-audit review, UnitReview approval, qualified Compare or claims, Finance posting/payment, supplier delivery, whole Procurement UAT, or M6B production activation. The owner receipts are scoped native persistence reconciliation, not a claim that every retained digest was independently recomputed.
