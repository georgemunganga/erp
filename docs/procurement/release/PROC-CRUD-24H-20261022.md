confirmation. Original-unit review establishes document evidence only; it
is not invoice approval, qualified financial matching or payment authority.

The eventual Finance adapter must supply scoped, authenticated, versioned
budget/reservation/release and AP posting/status contracts, idempotency and
reconciliation. Until then, required financial workflow stages remain
explicitly unavailable or pending their documented controlled evidence
route; ordinary permitted master/draft CRUD does not require an invented
Finance success or an employee link. This is a dependency, not a direction
to implement Finance inside Procurement.

## Delivery integration audit — 22 October 2026

Procurement event persistence is present, but the running delivery process
uses HRM's queue, subjects and templates. A healthy HRM publisher is not
proof of Procurement event or notification delivery. The bounded
[shared-delivery integration plan](PROC-OUTBOX-SHARED-DELIVERY-INTEGRATION-PLAN.md)
records the actual gaps and reuse boundary, finite queue-only privileges,
tenant/company routing, deduplication, retry/deadletter recovery, and
explicit backlog activation. No Procurement publisher, stream, email,
role or production configuration was enabled by this source audit.
